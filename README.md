# invoicer

invoicer is an app for creating invoices and sending them to your clients.

## What the app does

invoicer lets you fill in your client's details, add what you're charging for, and turn it into a clean invoice in a few minutes. It also keeps track of which invoices are still waiting to be paid.

## Who's it for

invoicer is for freelancers and small business owners who need to bill clients without learning complicated accounting software. If you send a handful of invoices a month and want them to look professional, it's made for you.

## How it works

1. **Create an invoice**: add your client's details and the work you're charging for.
2. **Send it**: email the invoice to your client straight from invoicer.
3. **Mark it as paid**: when the money arrives, mark the invoice as paid so you always know who still owes you.
