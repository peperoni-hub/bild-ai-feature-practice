# invoicer privacy policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026, 21:52_

## What data invoicer stores

To create and keep track of your invoices, invoicer stores:

- **Your details**: your name or business name, address, email address, and your logo if you add one.
- **Your clients' details**: the names, addresses and email addresses you save for your clients.
- **Your invoices**: invoice numbers, dates, the work you charged for, prices, totals, and whether each invoice is paid, waiting or overdue.

invoicer doesn't store your bank card details, and it doesn't collect anything about how you use the app for ads or tracking.

## Who can see your data

- **You**: you can see and change everything you've saved in invoicer.
- **Your clients**: they only see the invoices you send them, nothing else.
- **Nobody else**: invoicer doesn't sell or share your data with anyone.
